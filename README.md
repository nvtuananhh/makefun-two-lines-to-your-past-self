## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes
a two-line warning to `/output/entry.txt` — one sentence warning
your past self about a specific moment, then one sentence saying
why that moment mattered and what changed because of it:

> Don't tell your dad 'I'll call you back' on the night of December 23rd and then forget.
> He waited by the phone until midnight, and you have been calling back every Sunday since.

The entry is 177 characters long. Line 1 names one concrete moment —
a sentence said out loud on a specific night, December 23rd — not a
vague phase of life. Line 2 traces real cause and effect: he waited
by the phone until midnight, and the forgotten call became a weekly
ritual, calling back every Sunday since. The twist sharpens the
moment instead of decorating it: the call you forgot is the reason
you never forget one now. The voice is plain and vivid,
written to one specific person rather than to an audience.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
